Australian Culture

Julio Cesar Prava / BiblioVerse

A guide to understand Australia and Australians: famous Aussies, cities, food, slang dictionary, key dates, brands and curiosities, in English and Portuguese.

Why it is not slop. A cultural guide with a point of view, not a generated listicle. Each entry links to a reference so the reader can check it.

Websites & web apps2026

Brazilian Culture

Julio Cesar Prava / BiblioVerse

A journey to understand Brazil and Brazilians, built for foreigners: people, cities, food, music, dates and curiosities, in English and Portuguese.

Why it is not slop. Local knowledge shaping what the model drafts. The site explains Brazil the way a Brazilian would to a friend, with references.

GOATs

Julio Cesar Prava / BiblioVerse

Who is the greatest athlete of all time in each sport? GOATs, legends, stars and prospects across more than 300 disciplines, each with sources and a verification level.

Why it is not slop. A ranking site that shows its confidence level on every pick. The opposite of a generated top-ten with no sources.

Websites & web apps2026

Types of Rock

Julio Cesar Prava / BiblioVerse

The theory of evolution applied to rock music: 97 genres on an interactive tree with history, sound, genealogy and the greatest artists of each.

Why it is not slop. A static site generated from data files, so every fact lives in one place and is checked by a person before it reaches the page.

CA2023
Websites & web apps2023

Chatbot Arena

LMSYS (UC Berkeley)

Two anonymous chatbots answer the same prompt, you pick the better one, and millions of such votes produce the leaderboard the industry watches.

Why it is not slop. The most honest measure of AI quality is a human who does not know which model is talking. The site made that a habit.

Websites & web apps2023

Make Real

tldraw

Draw a rough interface on a whiteboard, press one button, and a working prototype appears next to your sketch, ready to click.

Why it is not slop. Drawing stayed the interface. The model fills in the code, the human keeps the pen.

C2022
Websites & web apps2022

Contexto

Nildo Junior

The Brazilian answer to Semantle: guess the secret word by meaning, ranked by a language model, which became the big word game of early 2023 far beyond Brazil.

Why it is not slop. A better version of someone else's idea, made for a language the original ignored. The ranking is friendlier than the score, and the world adopted it.

CM2022
Websites & web apps2022

Craiyon (DALL·E mini)

Boris Dayma

The free, open image generator that produced the wonky nine-image grids which flooded social media in mid-2022, months before the big models opened up.

Why it is not slop. The project that let the public touch image generation first. Its ugliness was part of the charm, and it was never sold as more than it was.

GF2022
Websites & web apps2022

Google Flood Hub

Google Research

A public site with river flood forecasts up to seven days ahead, built for regions without gauges, covering eighty countries since 2023.

Why it is not slop. A warning you can act on, in places that never had one. The site shows uncertainty and the forecasts are checked by people who sign off on them.

HI2022
Websites & web apps2022

Have I Been Trained?

Spawning (Mat Dryhurst & Holly Herndon)

A search engine that lets artists check whether their work is among the billions of images used to train image generators, and opt out of future training.

Why it is not slop. AI infrastructure turned toward the people it was built on. Consent as a product, before any law required it.

S2022
Websites & web apps2022

Semantle

David Turner

A daily word game where every guess gets a score for how close its meaning is to the secret word, computed by a word-embedding model.

Why it is not slop. A game that could only exist with a language model, and that uses it for one thing: a number. Nothing generated, nothing to look at, just meaning as a mechanic.

TI2022
Websites & web apps2022

The Infinite Conversation

Giacomo Miceli

An endless, never-repeating conversation between synthetic versions of Werner Herzog and Slavoj Žižek, with a new segment added every day.

Why it is not slop. A piece about the plausibility of nonsense, made before the chatbot boom. It works because the audience already expected both men to be hard to follow.

BO2020
Websites & web apps2020

Blob Opera

David Li with Google Arts & Culture

Four blobs sing opera as you drag them up and down, harmonising in real time with voices learned from sixteen hours of four real singers.

Why it is not slop. Joyful, instant and honest about what you hear: not the singers, but a machine's idea of them. Still the most played machine-learning toy on the web.

TP2019
Websites & web apps2019

This Person Does Not Exist

Phillip Wang

A single page that shows a new photorealistic face on every reload, none of which belongs to a real person.

Why it is not slop. The site is a demonstration of a risk, built to make it visible. Minimal, honest and still the best explainer of synthetic media.

QD2016
Websites & web apps2016

Quick, Draw!

Google Creative Lab

A game that asks you to doodle an object in twenty seconds while a neural network guesses what it is, which quietly collected the largest doodle dataset in the world.

Why it is not slop. It taught millions what a classifier is without a single explanation, and gave the data back to the public.
