How we choose

Three questions. A project needs a good answer to all of them.

  1. 01

    Is it useful?

    It solves a problem, teaches something, moves someone or entertains on purpose. Being generated is not a reason to exist.

  2. 02

    Is it innovative?

    It shows a way of using AI that was not obvious before: as a mechanic, a restoration tool, a collaborator, a microphone.

  3. 03

    Is it free of slop?

    A person chose, cut, checked and signed it. No default aesthetics, no unreviewed text, no waxy faces nobody looked at twice.

What is slop?

Slop is AI output published without judgement: the plastic skin, the six-finger hand, the paragraph nobody read, the site that looks like every other site. It is not a style, it is the absence of one. Every project here had a human who said no to the first result.

Three degrees of AI

Every entry says how much the machine did, because the honest answer is part of what makes it good.

  • AI-assisted

    AI was a support tool. The work would exist without it, just slower.

  • Co-created

    Human and AI in dialogue. Neither could have made this alone.

  • Human-directed

    AI produced most of the material; a person chose, cut, corrected and signed it.
