Oasis

A playable Minecraft-like world with no game engine: every frame is generated by a model from your keyboard and mouse input, in real time.

Where the human came in

The teams trained and released the model, published a smaller version openly and were upfront that the world drifts and forgets what is behind you.

Why it is not slop

Included as a landmark: the first widely played demo of a game that exists only inside a model. Its instability is the point, and the training data raised fair questions.
