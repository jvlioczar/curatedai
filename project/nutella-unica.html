Nutella Unica

Seven million jars, each with a unique label generated from a set of patterns and palettes designed by the agency.

NU2017
By
Ogilvy Italia & Ferrero
Year
2017
Country
Italy
Category
Graphic design & branding
Purpose
Commercial
Degree of AI
Human-directed
Tools
Generative algorithm

Where the human came in

Designers drew the graphic vocabulary and the rules; the algorithm only combined them. Every jar still looks like Nutella.

Why it is not slop

Generative design in service of a brand system. Variety without losing identity, which is what most AI design fails at.
