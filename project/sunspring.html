Sunspring

A nine-minute film whose screenplay was written entirely by a recurrent neural network and performed straight by human actors.

S2016
By
Oscar Sharp & Ross Goodwin
Year

Country

Tools

Where the human came in

Goodwin trained the model on sci-fi scripts; Sharp directed; Thomas Middleditch and the cast gave the nonsense emotional sense.

Why it is not slop

A deliberate experiment with the machine's incoherence, made before the hype, and still funnier than most AI films since.
