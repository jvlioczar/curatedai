Where the human came in

Herndon built and trained the model with Mat Dryhurst and Jules LaPlace, wrote the songs, and treated Spawn as one more voice in an ensemble.

Why it is not slop

The training data is consensual and the model is a collaborator with a credited role. The record sounds like nothing else.
